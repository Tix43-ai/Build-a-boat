Build a boat

Sail 2 km to the red flag. One motor makes your boat faster, but it needs fuel. Tap a fuel canister to pour its fuel into the motor. Spikes hurt the rock monsters, and rocks break the blocks they touch. Blocks left without support fall off.

Tutorial

1. Open the SHOP and open a chest to get blocks.

2. Pick a block button, then tap the grid to place it. Blocks drop until they land.

3. Place one Motor and fill it with Fuel canisters. Tap a canister to pour its fuel into the motor.

4. Press Launch to sail 2 km to the red flag.

5. Rocks break blocks, and spikes hurt rocks. Blocks cut off from the boat fall away.

6. Every 100 m sailed earns 10 coins, and winning gives 200 coins.
